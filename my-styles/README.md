# My styles

Styles made for this fork with the Lemo-Opuscar library's "make a style your own" flow (`MY-STYLES.md`). They live outside `styles/` and never change the library. Name one when you ask for a film ("Make a film in my Baked Light style about…").

| Style | 中文 | Slug | Based on | What it is | Folder |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Liminal Found Footage · Baked Light | 后室 · 烘焙光照 | `liminal-baked-light` | `backrooms` | Camcorder tapes from empty fluorescent interiors, with real baked light (three.js r187 `unwrapUVs` + `LightMapper`) that stops at walls and switches by layer. Demo: *Wing C* | [`my-styles/liminal-baked-light/`](liminal-baked-light/) |
| Documentary Parallax | 纪实视差 | `doc-parallax` | `archive-lighttable` (borrows Parallax Journey) | Real public-domain archive photographs turned into slow cinematic 3D scenes (depth bands from Depth Anything V2 Small, filled backgrounds, snow and dust in depth), handed over through objects (into the printed photo, whiteout, the route on a map), with editorial type and one red word. Demo: *Endurance* | [`my-styles/doc-parallax/`](doc-parallax/) |
