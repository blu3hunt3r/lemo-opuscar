# Documentary Parallax: decisions (from a blank page, technique base Archive Light Table; borrows Parallax Journey)

The user asked for this look from three references (a D-Day "3D parallax" clip and three Mirage demos: Apollo 11, Chernobyl, Black Tuesday), with public-domain archive images, and said "do it all": every decision below is my recommendation, taken without further questions.

| # | Section | Base (Archive Light Table) | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | §1 Essence | Archive objects on a backlit table; a loupe dives into them; catalogue truth | The same truth (real public-domain photos, sourced facts), but each photo becomes a 3D scene the camera enters; scenes hand over through objects; editorial type | The references' cinematic parallax, made honest: real archive instead of generated stills. Costs the glossy colour look of the references; the result reads as a documentary |
| 2 | §2 Materials | Positives/negatives on a light table; a ground-plane nearness map and hand-cut layers for "photograph space" | Every photo: monocular depth (Depth Anything V2 Small, offline) → 2–3 bands with filled backgrounds → meshes unprojected from depth; atmosphere particles; DoF, halation, grain, gentle grade | Depth for every photo without hand-cutting each one; moves limited so the fill never shows |
| 3 | §3 Colour, §4 Type | Table white, one signal orange; mono for identifiers, sans labels, serif titles; labels attached to objects | Archive tones graded as a whole; one signal red; four type roles: heavy sans title, mono date/tag, serif story line with one red italic word underlined, counting numbers | The editorial type system of the references, in OFL fonts; one red word = the meaning of each scene |
| 4 | §5 Motion, §6 Camera | Table glides, loupe landings and lifts, pushes into the lens | Slow pushes, drifts and rises inside photos; fast handovers: into a printed photo, whiteout, route on a map, pull back onto the page | Shots slow, transitions fast: the references' rhythm without cuts |
| 5 | §7 Sound, §8 Native moves | Light-table hum, loupe clacks, felt piano; negative → positive, the line lights up | Cinematic low strings, piano, drone, swells at the peaks; the place's air; signature **Into the printed photo**, plus whiteout, the route draws itself, the number lands, one word in red | A documentary score with a real silence before the turn; the signature turns the archive record into the event |
| 6 | §1, §2, §6, §8 (v2) | Catalogue truth only: real objects on the table, nothing staged | Still only real photographs and documents, never altered; plus **reconstructions** built in code for what no camera saw, tagged RECONSTRUCTION, with notes on what is sourced and what is invented; real period colour (Paget plates); the record on a lamp-lit desk; the flash, the shatter, flowing water, mist; LaMa inpainting for bigger moves | After comparing the first demo with the references (generated photoreal imagery), the user chose "a mix of both": the archive for what Hurley photographed, 3D for what no camera saw. Costs: reconstructions are CGI, not photographs, and take far longer to render |
