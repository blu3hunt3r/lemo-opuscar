# SOURCES v2: new archive assets for Endurance v2

This continues `SOURCES.md` (same columns, same rules). All files are in `assets/raw/`. Checked 31 Oct 2026. Nothing was retouched, colourised, upscaled or generated. The only changes were format conversion to JPEG (quality 95, 4:4:4), a colour-profile conversion to sRGB where stated, and, for book plates printed sideways on the page, turning the plate upright and trimming the page down to the plate plus a thin paper margin. Masters were downloaded at full size into `films/endurance-v2/_dl/` (scratch, deletable); the master URLs below let anyone fetch them again.

**Licence basis common to the Hurley photographs** (Frank Hurley, 1885-1962): see `SOURCES.md`. In short, photographs taken before 1 Jan 1955 are out of copyright in Australia (NLA: "Photographs taken before 1 January 1955 are all out of copyright.", https://www.library.gov.au/copyright-library-collections/rights-and-pictures-collection). In the UK, photographs taken before 1 June 1957 had 50 years from the making of the negative, a term that ran out in the 1960s. Anything published in the US before 1931 is public domain there.

## What the v2 film uses

`p1` (the flash), `colour2` (the colour plate; `p2` stands in when its layers are missing), `p3` (the wreck), `p5` (the launch: the 1916 Underwood print, whose white surf the book plate `p5_hi` lacks), `p6_hi` (the beach: Hurley's own lantern slide, trimmed to 2692 × 1653 inside the rounded slide mask, left 3 %, top 12 %, right 3 %, bottom 9 %; the LoC print `p6` from `SOURCES.md` stands in when its layers are missing), `map1919` with `map_control.json` and `map_boatline.json` (the printed "Boat Journey" line, traced on the scan), `news`, and `p2` and `p4` as prints on the desk; `route.json` and `land.geojson` (the route's geometry and the fallback paper map). Collected but not used: `colour3`, `p5_hi`, `p6_hi_book`, and from the first version `page`, `portrait` and `p7`.

## Not used, rejected, or still missing

1. **The 1919 first printing of the same map** (IA `South00Shac`, Museum Victoria copy, leaf 493: 5469 × 5551, JP2 only 0.9 MB): more pixels, but soft and washed out from heavy JP2 compression. A side-by-side crop showed the 1920 printing's scan (`map1919.jpg`) is sharper even when enlarged to the same scale. It is the same plate ("London Wm Heinemann", Stanford). Not used.
2. **"Launching the James Caird" at ≥ 2500 px from a glass plate or lantern slide: none found.** The LoC has only the 1536 px copy negative (`p5.jpg`, LC-USZ62-9523), and an LoC search for "james caird" found no other print. Commons has no SLNSW scan of it, only `South - the story of Shackleton's last expedition, 1914-1917 - Launching the James Caird.jpg` (1962 × 1133, a book-plate scan, smaller than `p5_hi`) and `LaunchingTheJamesCaird2.jpg` (1332 × 822, no holder). `p5_hi.jpg` is therefore the book half-tone.
3. **Wikimedia Commons downloads** (upload.wikimedia.org) answered **HTTP 429** ("Too many requests") to 11 of the 12 file requests from this session's shared IP; only `AllSafeAllWell.jpg` came through. Requests were serial, at least 10 s apart, with a 5-minute pause after every 429 and at most 6 tries per file. The Commons API (commons.wikimedia.org) answered 3 of 6 attempts, which is how the file pages below were read. See the bottom of this file for each file's state.
4. **Other Hurley Paget plates on Flickr**: the State Library posted about 19 Paget plates in 2009. Checked one by one, their Flickr originals are 1200 px (too small), except "Dog teams scouting a way to the land" (now 6624 × 4656, `colour3.jpg`). It also posted ON 26/16 at full size in 2024 (`colour2.jpg`). "The 'Endurance' frozen in 76-35 South" (3534625867) now returns 404 on Flickr.
5. **SLNSW catalogue, NLA/Trove and web.archive.org** were not reachable from this session. The first two turn away automated clients (see `SOURCES.md`), and web.archive.org closed every connection. I did not try to get round them.
